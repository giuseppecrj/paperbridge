# PAPERBRIDGE :D

## ISSUE #44 REVIEW TICKET

**Prototype ticket cooperation**

**Status:** In progress

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS
---

We need evidence that one normal Markdown file is clear and controllable when a human and an agent edit it together.

## GOAL
---

Use this document as the shared artifact for one editing pass. Keep human edits intact and review the result without a compiler or Verifier.

## CURRENT DECISION
---

- The file is ordinary Markdown.
- Front matter identifies it as a Paperbridge receipt.
- The Markdown body is the receipt.
- The human has final authority over the content.

## REVIEW CHECKLIST
---

- [ ] The source is understandable in an ordinary text editor.
- [ ] The likely printed content is obvious without rendering it.
- [ ] Human edits remain visible and intact.
- [ ] The document feels useful without deterministic tooling.

## OUT OF SCOPE
---

- Defining final print syntax.
- Compiling to Paperbridge Blocks.
- Contacting the Device or Printer.
