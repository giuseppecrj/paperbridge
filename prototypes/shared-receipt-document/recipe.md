# {name}

**Serves:** {serves}

---

## 1. WARM

- {milk}

Heat the milk until warm, about 120 F. Do not boil it.

---

## 2. MELT

- {chocolate}

Melt the chocolate gently. Stir until smooth.

---

## 3. TEMPER

Whisk one spoonful of warm milk into the chocolate. Repeat until the mixture loosens, then add the milk in gradually larger amounts.

---

## 4. FINISH

- {vanilla}
- {sugar}
- {salt}

Whisk in the remaining ingredients. Add the last of the milk, warm to the desired serving temperature, and serve.
